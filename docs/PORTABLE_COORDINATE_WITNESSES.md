# C0.5 — Portable coordinate witnesses and storage seals

The historical block pathway exposes a durable store identity and portable observations of retained revisions. E-CPU's unchanged PageGroundBacking continues to realize leaf bytes. A separate JavaScript verifier does not require Python, SQLite, the original page representation or a live worker. This is a storage Ground qualification, not a whole-machine snapshot or a VGI certificate interoperability claim.

## Receipt and witness

A local store UUID is inserted once in a SQLite identity table. Existing pre-identity databases migrate on open without rewriting their historical roots. Migration establishes new identity; previously issued receipts without identity are not accepted by the new verifier. A database copied in full also copies identity: the UUID is a lineage identifier, not a unique physical host or exclusive authority. No signatures or anti-rollback guarantee.

A committed receipt binds Ground version vlk.coordinate-ground.v1, identity (32 lowercase hex), geometry in pages, database-local revision and coordinate root. The live device receipt additionally binds branch and dirty status. The root law is unchanged: sentinel for all-zero subtrees, native SHA-256 at nonzero leaves, SHA-256 of domain bytes VLK-node-v1 plus NUL, one-byte level and ordered 32-byte child digests at internal nodes. Canonical zero collapse is required. Root alone does not authenticate store identity, revision, geometry or authority.

HistoryDevice.witness(revision, coordinate) requests an independently readable committed page, a receipt and one sibling digest per tree level, ordered top-down. It deliberately excludes pending uncommitted writes. The trusted local worker allows any retained revision in the store. verifyWitness(witness, expectedReceipt, expectedCoordinate) validates the caller-pinned receipt, geometry, coordinate, canonical base64 of one 4096-byte page and path shape, then reconstructs the root bottom-up. Returns the observed bytes or refuses. It does not trust the receipt supplied by the witness as its own authority. For 16384 logical pages, the path carries 14 digests (448 binary bytes; 896 hex characters on wire) in addition to one page and metadata. Verification and generation traverse at most depth nodes; no disk scan or replay. Verification proves membership relative to a trusted root, not authenticity of that root. No selective sub-page disclosure or nonexistence of external effects is implied.

## Durable storage seal

HistoryDevice.seal() is a queued flush returning the clean receipt from that precise successful publication boundary; later writes do not alter the returned object. HistoryDevice.open(path,{branch,expected:seal}) refuses if identity, Ground version, geometry, revision, root, branch or committed cleanliness differ. A same-root alternative branch is also refused. Open pins the selected revision, not an exclusive writer lease; later publication remains guarded by CAS. This seal does not implement upstream snapshotState/snapshotSeal: the upstream API requires a synchronous generation description at the parked machine boundary, including the correct device and whole-machine consistency. An async flush seal alone cannot satisfy that contract. No upstream ABI change.
